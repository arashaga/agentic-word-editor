    About this assistant

    No message-count limit. Long conversations use recent messages and the current document for model context. Older messages remain in the chat until cleared or the session expires.

    Supports text, font family, size, colors, bold/italic, alignment, and paragraph-to-table conversion. Fonts are not embedded; Word may substitute unavailable fonts.

    Messages, recent history and supported document content go to your configured Azure model. Public web search is billable preview; queries/results may leave Azure geographic/compliance boundaries under Bing terms. Word document contents are not sent to web search. Do not enter secrets.
